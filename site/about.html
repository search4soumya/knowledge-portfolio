Who I am

About me

With over a decade of experience in software engineering, I specialize in designing and implementing large-scale distributed systems that power enterprise applications. My journey has taken me through the evolution of software architecture, from monolithic applications to microservices, from bare-metal servers to cloud-native Kubernetes deployments.

I believe that great architecture is not just about technology choices, but about understanding business domains, anticipating change, and creating systems that are as elegant as they are robust. Every pattern I implement, every service I design, is crafted with maintainability, scalability, and developer experience in mind.

When I'm not architecting systems, I share my knowledge through technical writing and mentoring. This space is my digital garden, a collection of insights, patterns, and lessons learned from years of building software that matters.
